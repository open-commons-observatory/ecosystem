# OCO

The open commons' ecosystem, as a constellation: a place of its own that keeps what holds between its
stars - **ocoeco** (the marks, the lenses, the acts), **oco-agent** (the coordinator of work, in
`creation-guidelines/lab-agent` until it takes its own name) and **tad** (text as data).

OCO is the ecosystem, not an observatory: anyone may have an observatory, and look at OCO as one
constellation among those they track. What is true of one star is kept in that star; what is true
between them is kept here.

Standing here, `ocoeco fulldome` shows OCO: this place and the stars it gathers.
